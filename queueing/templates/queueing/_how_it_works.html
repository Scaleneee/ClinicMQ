How the queue works

  1. One line for everyone. Online and walk-in numbers share the same queue.
  2. Check in when you arrive. Only checked-in patients are called, smallest number first.
  3. Come when it's near. We'll tell you to come when {{ cs.near_alert_threshold }} or fewer people are ahead of you.
  4. Late? If a later number is called before you arrive, you're skipped. When you check in, you go after the next {{ cs.grace_count }} patients.
  5. Called but not there? After {{ cs.miss_timeout_min }} minutes you're marked as missed. You can check in again once; missing it a second time cancels your number.
  6. Numbers end with the session. Unused numbers expire when the session closes.
